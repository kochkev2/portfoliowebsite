Kevin is a game designer, developer, and pixel artist studying Game Design and Spanish at Michigan State University

About

My interest in game development comes from wanting to create experiences that are fun, memorable, and especially meaningful to players. I have worked on projects individually and as part of multidisciplinary teams, taking on roles in 2D and 3D design, UI/UX design, production, leadership, and development.

I am very fond of pixel art and am always actively working to improve my own pixel style for the games I design.

I currently lead Clustre Studios, an independent game studio with 10+ members, where we are working on releasing several games by December.

One day, I hope to be recognized for my art style in games and make fun and unique experiences that encourage the next generation of designer!

Projects

Tinted Isle

Tinted Isle is a strategic point-and-click gardening simulator about discovering and growing unique plants on an island. Players grow plants of different colors, trade with a diverse cast of visiting merchants, expand their island, and discover increasingly rare plants.

Behind the Design

One of the biggest challenges in designing Tinted Isle has been maintaining a spreadsheet containing more than 280 unique plant combinations and their effects.

I create all of the game's artwork and am one of its two main designers on a six-person team. Tinted Isle is planned for release in December 2026.

Project Details

  • Role: Artist and Game Designer
  • Team: 6 people
  • Tools: Unity, C#, Google Spreadsheets, Aseprite, Pixquare

ConSwaption

ConSwaption is a physical memory card game where players match ingredients to craft strategic potions and defeat rival wizards. It combines the familiarity of a matching game with additional strategy through deciding which potions to create and how to use them against opponents.

As part of a nine-person team, I created all of the game's artwork and contributed to its design and playtesting. We developed the game through paper prototyping before producing a finished physical version. ConSwaption was also shown at the 2026 MSU CAS Spring Games Showcase and is currently being ordered through The Game Crafter.

Project Details

  • Role: Artist, Game Designer, and Playtester
  • Team: 9 people
  • Tools: Aseprite, Pixquare, paper prototyping, The Game Crafter

Hive Blinds

Hive Blinds is a strategic trick-taking card game about using political prowess to influence four hivemind races fighting for control of a planet. Players compete to manipulate the conflict and position themselves to come out on top.

This is a two-person project where I create all of the artwork and share the design and playtesting responsibilities. We designed Hive Blinds so that the complete game can be played at home using a standard 52-card deck. The physical game is complete, with the goal of developing a digital version this year.

Project Details

  • Role: Artist, Game Designer, and Playtester
  • Team: 2 people
  • Tools: Aseprite, Pixquare, Tabletop Simulator

Shroom to Breathe

Shroom to Breathe is a time-management collectathon and mental wellness app that rewards players for getting work done. Players set timers while they work and collect little mushroom creatures along the way. They can breed, organize, and discover new shrooms while using the app to stay productive.

I am one of two developers on the project and am responsible for the artwork as well as the design of the game's systems and user interface. My work covers essentially every part of the experience outside of programming and sound. Shroom to Breathe is planned for release on the App Store this winter.

Project Details

  • Role: Artist, Game Designer, and UI/UX Designer
  • Team: 2 people
  • Tools: Unity, C#, Aseprite, Pixquare

Fish Flop

Fish Flop is a 2.5D pixel-art puzzle platformer where two miniature divers navigate a series of fish tanks. Players rotate around each tank to change the perspective of the level and flip aquarium decorations to solve puzzles, open new paths, and progress to the next tank.

On our seven-person team, I created all of the 2D artwork and helped prototype and design about half of the game's levels. The project combines my pixel art with a 3D environment, making the interaction between its 2D and 3D elements an important part of both the visual design and gameplay. A 15-level demo is currently available on itch.io.

Project Details

  • Role: 2D Artist and Level Designer
  • Team: 7 people
  • Tools: Unity, Blender, Aseprite, Pixquare
