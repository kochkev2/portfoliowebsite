Kevin is a game designer, developer, and pixel artist studying Games & Interactive Media and Spanish at Michigan State University

About

My interest in game development comes from wanting to create experiences that are fun, memorable, and especially meaningful to players. I have worked on projects individually and as part of multidisciplinary teams, taking on roles in 2D and 3D design, UI/UX design, production, leadership, and development.

I am very fond of pixel art and am always actively working to improve my own pixel style for the games I design.
